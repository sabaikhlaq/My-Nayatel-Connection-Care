Check the fibre cable

  1. Find the thin cable going into the port marked PON or Fibre.
  2. Push it in until it clicks. Make sure it isn't bent sharply.
  3. Wait 1 minute, then look at the lights.

What you should see

LOS light off and PON light steady. If LOS is blinking red, the cable may be damaged.
